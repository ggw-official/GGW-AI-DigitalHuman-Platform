# GGW AI Digital Human Platform

## Overview

GGW AI Digital Human Platform is an AI-powered content creation and digital human management platform designed to simplify the entire content production workflow.

The platform enables users to create AI-generated scripts, synthesize realistic voices, manage digital avatars, and generate professional digital human content from a single interface.
